description: Rules for the judges' slides deck — brand source and how product status and problem numbers may be phrased.

Deck text pulls app name, tagline, logo and demo identities from BRAND, even though the slides editor prefers static text.

**Why:** the single-brand-source rule outranks slide-editor editability for those spans.

Until a flow is verified working on testnet, slides must label it as planned (future tense, "planned"/"expected"), not merely "testnet-only". A reviewer rejected present-tense demo/architecture wording as overclaiming.

**Why:** judges' credibility; the repo's rule is to verify before describing a flow as working.

**How to apply:** every statistic needs a visible source line and the "safer phrasing" from the research doc's weak-spots table (World Bank 15% = small $200 transfers, never tuition; never say kids' cards fail abroad). Flip planned wording to present tense only after the flow is confirmed.

**Judges see the video, not the deck (user decision, 2026-10-24).** The 90-second demo video is the only pitch material judges will see, so it must carry the problem numbers, the four features, how it runs on testnet and the testnet disclaimer. Keep the video script's coverage aligned with any deck change.
